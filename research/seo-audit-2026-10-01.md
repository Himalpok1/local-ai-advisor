## Fixed in the follow-up commit

- **Trust pages:** `/about`, `/privacy` (Google Analytics, AdSense, Google sign-in, community reports, deletion), `/terms`, `/contact`, linked from every page footer and added to the sitemap.
- **Engine accuracy:**
  - The Chat and Long-documents ratings assumed a full developer setup (6 GB of other apps), so every 8 GB Mac showed "0 models". They now assume a browser (3 GB), like a casual user would have.
  - Small machines now show what runs with other apps closed. `/learn/first-model` matches this.
  - The quantization pick no longer chooses 3-bit when a 4-bit-or-better version is usable.
  - "Doesn't fit" answers now quote the smallest version's memory.
- **"Doesn't fit" pages** now explain the exact memory gap and list concrete ways to make it work: close other apps, a smaller model from the same family, the same chip with more memory, and the cheapest machines. They also have honest meta descriptions. They stay indexable.
- **Structured data:**
  - Organization, WebSite and WebApplication on the homepage.
  - BreadcrumbList on every can-i-run, what-runs-on, blog and info page.
  - BlogPosting is linked to the blog and the publisher, with Ray credited as the site's writer.
- **Social previews:** site-wide Open Graph and Twitter defaults plus a branded share image, so every page gets a title, description and image. `/hf` pages keep their own image and alt text.
- **Canonicals** on the homepage, `/check`, `/learn` and every lesson, the catalogs, the compare pages, `/methodology`, `/tools`, `/runtimes`, `/stack` and `/hardware-for-model`. `/evaluate` is now `noindex, follow`.
- **Security headers:** HSTS, `X-Content-Type-Options` and `Referrer-Policy`; `X-Powered-By` removed.
- **AI discovery:** `/llms.txt`, generated from the site's own data.
- **Bugs:**
  - Mobile horizontal scroll on the ~4,500 model-and-computer pages.
  - Lessons showing "Up next · lesson 0".
  - "Too slow" shown when the real issue wasn't speed.
  - The header logo was lazy-loaded.
